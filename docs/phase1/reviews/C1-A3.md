# C1 `otsec.asset/0.1`: A3 security review

Reviewer: A3. Date: 2026-10-24. Reviewed: PR #32, commit 946cb7b (`contracts/asset/`). The review covers security-sensitive behaviour. It focuses on the B-05 statement, the `downstream_security` labelling and the identity binding, and on whether anything in C1 lets a device's own claims raise trust.

**Outcome: accepted with changes C1-A3-1 to C1-A3-4, all needed before merge.** C1-A3-5 is a C7 and C8 consequence that A3 takes on. C1-A3-6 and C1-A3-7 are notes.

## B-05 statement: accepted

The text in `doc.go` says what the binding proves, what it does not, and what a mismatch does. It does not imply authentication:

- The binding is `device-reported` and labelled `spoofable`, a match "never authenticates the device", and the password still goes in clear text to whatever answers.
- The FID check is the only pre-authentication check, and the serial number is checked only after the password has been sent. The text states that ordering plainly.
- A mismatch never resolves automatically, and the observed value never replaces the declared one. A conflicting attribute keeps the operator-declared value, so a device claim cannot overwrite the records.

One addition, in C1-A3-4: the doc should also say that a passive listener on the station LAN can read the password. The binding gives no protection there, and that is the main reason Telnet is lab-only.

## C1-A3-5: consequence of the serial-number ordering (A1's question)

**Decision: yes, it needs a C7 and C8 consequence, and A3 takes it on.** A serial mismatch after login means the Level 1 password went to a device that is not the bound one. The credential must be treated as exposed, not just paused.

1. **C7 credential state.** The collector suspends the credential reference with reason `identity-mismatch`. Unlike `auth-failed`, this suspension **cannot be cleared on its own**. It is cleared only by one of two things. Either the operator replaces the credential (a new version, which is rotation), or the asset is re-commissioned with a new declared serial, recorded after the suspension, with the operator stating that the new device is legitimate. Otherwise `credential.clear_suspension` is refused with `credential_unusable`.
2. **No events from the wrong device.** The serial is compared from the SER report header, so no extra command is needed. `STA` is not added, because its siblings `STA C` and `STA R` restart the relay. On a mismatch the adapter stops before any row becomes an event. The C2 transcript is kept as evidence and labelled `identity_mismatch`. No C4 event is created for the asset from that session.
3. **Close.** At that point the session is at Level 1, so `QUI` and close are safe (D18), as C1 already says.
4. **C8 records.** Three records are written: `device.refused` with reason `identity_mismatch`, `credential.suspend` with reason `identity_mismatch` naming the credential version, and an alarm.

C7 aligns its suspend-reason names with C1's list (`auth-failed`, `password-prompt-absent`, `unexpected-access-level`, `identity-mismatch`), so the asset view and the credential view use the same words.

## Notes, no change needed

- **C1-A3-6:** asset IDs are assigned and never derived from device content, with a negative example. Simulator evidence cannot be shown as a device observation. Unknown values from a later minor fall back to the least trusted value. All three hold.
- **C1-A3-7:** capability profiles ship inside the signed register package, so a profile cannot widen what the collector sends (C-A2-2). C7's `VerifyRegisterPackage` covers them automatically, as long as they are inside the payload. Please confirm the profile file's location when C3 content lands.

## Re-check of the fixes (04e0b45, 2026-10-24)

A3 read the diff and ran the package tests at b50d7bf (PR #32 head). All pass. Each new negative example is pinned to its expected error text, so none can pass by failing for an unrelated reason.

- **C1-A3-1: closed.** `AlwaysPlaintext` covers Telnet, serial and Syslog without TLS. A serial endpoint labelled anything else fails, with a negative example.
- **C1-A3-2: closed.** `encrypted-authenticated` fails unless the binding method is `pinned-key`, with negatives for SSH and `syslog-tls`. A consequence to keep in mind: an SSH link without a pinned key is `encrypted-weak`, and its acknowledgement can only carry `lab-only` in 0.1. So in 0.1, any link that does not authenticate the device is lab-only. That is the intended reading of ADR-0005. A production scope needs an owner decision and a later minor.
- **C1-A3-3: closed on the C1 side.** `Acknowledgement.Scope` exists with the single value `lab-only`, and a missing scope fails. `By` is marked TODO(C7) as a human principal ID. C7 refuses a run with `credential_mismatch` when the credential's `plaintext_scope` differs from the endpoint acknowledgement's scope. That rule belongs to the collector and is documented in C7.
- **C1-A3-4: closed.** `doc.go` has the passive-capture sentence, and it also states the post-login mismatch consequence (C1-A3-5).

**C1-A3-5 alignment.** C7's credential suspend reasons are now `auth-failed`, `password-prompt-absent`, `unexpected-access-level`, `identity-mismatch` and `operator`, spelled as in C1. C1's other two reasons, `language-not-english` and `firmware-not-allowlisted`, are found in the probe phase before `ACC`. No password has been sent at that point, so they suspend the asset but not the credential. Only `identity-mismatch` cannot be cleared on the same credential version.

**Outcome now: accepted.**

## D1-24 endpoint target at `0cad862` (PR #65): accepted

A2's change makes `Endpoint.target` (`device-observed` or `simulated`) required, cross-checks observations against the asset's endpoints, adds C2 `Result.CheckAgainst`, and corrects the floor comments in C2 for the D1-20 amendment.
- At `0cad862`, `gofmt -l contracts`, `go vet ./contracts/...` and `go test ./contracts/...` all pass.
- Evidence class: `desk`.

What I checked:
- A missing or unknown target is refused in every minor and never defaulted. `later-minor-endpoint-target-unknown` covers it.
- The target is set by an admin, and never taken from the far end.
- No C1, C2 or C7 rule is relaxed for a `simulated` endpoint: the plaintext acknowledgement, the credential rules and the identity binding all apply as before. This is the property that matters. Labelling a real relay "simulated" must never buy a weaker session. Please keep it that way in later minors.
- `Result.CheckAgainst` refuses a result whose target differs from its endpoint's. A simulator session therefore cannot be ingested as a device read (P10).
- The C2 floor comments in `codes.go` and `dialogue.go` now say the floor starts at ACC. They match C7 in #64.
