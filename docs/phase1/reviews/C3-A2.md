## Answers to A1's questions

**1. Known FIDs: 80 in the package; Appendix A has 78 unique FIDs in 82 rows.**

Tables A.1 to A.5 have 82 rows:
- **79 rows carry a full FID.** They hold 78 unique strings: `SEL-751-R200-V4-Z007003-D20170922` appears in two rows, on Table A.4 sheets 3 and 4. The two rows have different change lists and manual date codes (20171206 and 20170922). The manual has one FID for two descriptions, so a relay reporting that FID cannot be told apart. Branch R200 is denied, so this does not affect collection.
- **3 rows have no FID:** R200-V1, R200-V0 and R111 are listed as "was not released".

Per branch, the 78 FIDs are:
- one each for R101, R102, R103, R105, R106, R108, R109, R110;
- R112: 4, R113: 3, R200: 3, R201: 2, R202: 6;
- R300: 10, R301: 6, R302: 10;
- R400: 10, R401: 8, R402: 5;
- R500: 2, R501: 1.

The package has two strings that are not Appendix A rows:
- `SEL-751-R100-V0-Z001001-D20070410` comes from the Figure 10.16 SER example (p. 10.40), a 2007 example in a 2026 manual (C-20).
- `SEL-751-R100-V0-Z001001-D20110601` is the sample FID in the Appendix A introduction (p. A.1), which explains the FID format. It is not a release row. The first listed release is `SEL-751-R101-V0-Z001001-D20110601` (Table A.5).

Both are on branch R100, which is denied, so neither changes a decision. But both carry the citation "Appendix A, firmware revision history", which is wrong for them. I cannot reach A6's count of 84. A6 should share its row list so the difference can be named: 84 is 2 more than the 82 rows I find, and 6 more than the unique FIDs.

**2. `sel.ser.other_args` citation.** Replace the pseudo-source `A2-P0` with S22 Table 7.63 (p. 7.75). That table documents the argument forms `SER row1`, `SER row1 row2`, `SER date1`, `SER date1 date2` (date format per `DATE_F`), `SER C` and `SER R` (clear), and Table 7.64 documents `SER D`. The rule denies every argument form, including documented read-only ones, until a form is approved as its own operation. So its classification should be `unknown`, not `state-changing`: the documented range forms are read-only, but they are not approved. Its evidence should be `documented-untested`.

**3. Session-line phases: confirmed.**
- `ACC` is sent in auth only.
- `QUI` is sent in probe (the Level 0 language probe, ADR-0005) and in close.
- In close, `QUI` is sent after every successful session. It is also sent after an unexpected access level (`=>>`), and after `ACC` reached `=>` with no password prompt (C2 proposal, see `docs/phase1/A2-contracts.md`): in that case the relay is at Level 1 and not inside a password prompt, so `QUI` is not a password attempt.
- `QUI` is never sent after a rejected password (C-19).

## Checked and agreed

- **Session lines.** `ACC`, `QUI` as `session-control` with phases, never returned by `Matcher.Operation`.
- **Operations.** `ID`, `SER`, `SHO G` and `SHO P 1` are exact literal lines with no parameters. Access levels are 0, 1, 1 and 1, which match Tables 7.43, 7.63 and 7.67.
- **Deny rules.**
  - They are case-insensitive and tolerate abbreviations: `(?i)\s*SER\w*\s+C\b.*` catches `SER C`, `ser c` and `SERX C`.
  - Every approved line is checked against them.
  - `sel.ser.d` stays denied (Spanish `SER D` clears the SER).
  - `sel.cse.candidate` stays denied (A1 deferral).
- **Firmware branch rules.** These match `docs/phase0/A2-sel751-firmware-allowlist.json`:
  - R501 and R500 allow from V0.
  - R402 V2, R401 V5 and R400 V7 allow, with condition `EHTTP := N`.
  - Everything older is denied.
  - The allowed entries are classed `inferred`, and the package notes record the owner's acceptance. C1 and C3 should agree on one token for "inferred, owner-accepted": C1 profiles use `inferred-owner-accepted`, and C3 uses `inferred` with a note. I am happy to switch C1 to C3's form when it is imported (TODO(C1) in `register.go`).
- **Unknown firmware fails closed.** Matching is exact on the full FID and needs a branch rule, and the `not_covered` list names the SEL-751A and later releases.
