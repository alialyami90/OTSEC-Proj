## Checked and agreed

- **Row identity.** `native_position` carries the relay's row number verbatim and is not used as identity. On the SEL-751 the row number is positional, with row 1 the newest (Table 7.63, C-06).
- **Ordering.** The chronological sequence numbers (row 8 is seq 1) and the two rows sharing `13:54:09.602` staying two events match the figure and A6 C-13.
- **Offset.** `offset: unknown` with no UTC is right. SER times are relay local time, and `UTC_OFF` applies only under SNTP (Table 4.90 text). An offset of `device-setting` needs `SHO G`, which is an approved register line.
- **Accuracy.** `accuracy_ns` nil with `quality.accuracy_relative_ns` = 1 ms is right: the ±1 ms is "with respect to time source" (S16).
- **Sync.** `sync: unknown` under `Time Source: Internal` is right. Table 7.16 says the field reports whether a time-code input is attached, not lock quality, and SNTP state is not printed.
- **Date format.** `date-format-assumed:MDY` until `DATE_F` is read is right (Table 7.16; `DAT` prints unpadded `7/29/2010`, so a parser must accept one-digit day and month).
- **Bytes.** Fields keep control bytes; `validate.go` does not reject them. SEL output can contain NUL and XON (Phase 0 §7), so this is what I need.
- **RTAC.** Taking the event time from MSG and keeping the HEADER time as a native field matches my portfolio finding (`A2-sel-portfolio.md` section 3). `origin_auth: none` for UDP is right.
- **ALIAS and `DATE_F`.** A change to `ALIAS` or `DATE_F` rewrites every row's bytes, so alignment reports a gap after such a change (p. 10.40). That is the conservative outcome, and the "Relay Settings Changed" row in the SER explains it.
